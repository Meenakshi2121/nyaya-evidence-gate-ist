# Prior Exploratory Analysis

An earlier exploratory analysis was conducted before the final framework and analysis plan were fixed.

The exploratory analysis used a different gate specification and produced preliminary findings, including approximately 47.1% gate coverage and no clear improvement over the treat-all-aspirin policy.

These findings informed the development of the present framework. They are not presented as results from a prospectively prespecified confirmatory analysis.

The final estimator set, gate definition, thresholds, matched-coverage comparator, bootstrap procedure and policy contrasts will be specified in `analysis-plan-v1.md`.

Any differences between the exploratory and final analyses will be reported transparently in the manuscript and supplementary material.
